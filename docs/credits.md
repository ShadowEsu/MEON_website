# Image credits and licences

Every image on the site that MEON didn't make itself, with where it came from and its licence. Checked on 27 Oct 2026.

## MEON's own

- **Noodle photos** (`src/assets/img/noodles/`): Steven's MEON photo shoot, commissioned by MEON, except the packet photos listed under "Noodle photos from free libraries" below. **Packet check, 31 Oct 2026:** each photo shows the packet next to the bowl, so a photo is only used on the noodle whose packet it shows. The photo and 3 extras filed as MyKuali Penang White Curry show an Ibumie Penang White Curry Mee packet, so they now live on that noodle (`ibumie-penang-white-curry-mee*.webp`). The Drive folder "Mama Shrimp Tomyum Flavour" holds the Wai Wai Tom Yum Shrimp bowl (the Wai Wai packet is in frame), so the home page's tom yum close-up (`shoot/tomyum-egg-prawn-*`) now links to Wai Wai Quick Zabb Tom Yum Shrimp. The photos this check took off the site were deleted from the repo on 1 Nov 2026 (they stay in git history): `samyang-samyang-ramen*.webp` (the packet is Samyang Bulgogi), `nissin-ramen-kyushu-black-garlic.webp` (the packet is Demae Iccho Black Garlic Oil Tonkotsu) and `more/mama-tom-yum-shrimp-1..4` (the Wai Wai bowl, the same shoot as that noodle's own photos).
- **Booth-shoot bowl photos** (`src/assets/img/shoot/`, used on the home page, Visit page and as the share image): MEON's own shoot on a black background (MEON's Drive, one folder per noodle), resized from the 4K originals. **Source check, 4 Nov 2026:** all 7 originals are camera files (Fujifilm X-T5, XF18-55mm lens, edited in Lightroom), shot on 6 and 7 Jan 2026, each folder with a frame of the bowl next to its own packet. They have no GPS, and the set (black tufted booth, round wooden table) isn't MEON's shop as its store-tour video shows it, so the home page doesn't say they were shot in the shop. `tonkotsu-top` and `tonkotsu-nori-close` are the same bowl, so the home grid uses only one of them.
- **Background videos** (`src/assets/video/`): MEON's own footage. The shop clips are Preston's; the step, wall and `ig-*.mp4` clips also use MEON's own @meonnoodles reels. Reels by other creators (for example the @little_miss_fussy_eats collab) are not re-hosted.
- **Topping photos on the MEON lid** (`toppings/meatballs.webp`, `toppings/spring-onion.webp`, `photos/topping-*.webp`): MEON's own topping shoot (MEON's Drive folder "Toppings pics"). **Not used since 31 Oct 2026:** the owner doesn't want photos on the black background, so those pairings showed line icons instead (`data/toppings.json`). The files were deleted from the repo on 1 Nov 2026 so they're no longer served; they stay in git history.
- **Pairing and side photos by the owner** (`src/assets/img/toppings/`, every file not in the tables below): MEON's owner photographed what the shop serves and sells on 30 Oct 2026 and sent the 47 files to Preston on 4 Nov 2026 through Google Drive (copies sorted into Preston's Drive folder "MEON photos: pairings, sides and drinks"; the originals stay in the owner's Drive). The photos are centred 480 × 480 square crops, saved without camera data (no EXIF; the originals have no location). Names follow the owner's file names: "Amy's Chicken" is the chicken wings, "Narutomaki" the fishcake, "Chesse Slice" the cheese slice. **Not used**, after a check of every photo for logos, packaging, watermarks and stock marks (4 Nov 2026): his 15 drink photos, because a can, bottle or pouch carries another company's packaging and logo (Preston via BOSS), so the drinks are listed as text; and "Seaweed.jpg", a branded packet of seasoned laver, so seasoned seaweed keeps its line icon. Items that are on the page because the owner's photos show the shop has them: fish balls, bean sprouts, Gabo nuggets, chicken sausage, mini frankfurts, sliced, penguin, Pandy and Thai fish cakes, fish balls with roe, lobster balls, crab nuggets, prawn wontons, cheese tofu, bok choy, spinach, quail eggs, cheese slice and kimchi, plus the drink Milkis Peach. He also photographed Red Bull and Milkis banana, melon and original, which aren't in the till: they stay off the page until he confirms they're on sale (Security, 4 Nov 2026). The owner's photos replaced the Commons beef, crab stick and tofu photos on 4 Nov 2026.
- **Logo** (`logo-*.png`, favicons): MEON's own.
- **Chilli in the spice meter** (`src/assets/img/chilli.svg`), mascots, icons and illustrated packets: drawn in code for this site (`scripts/lib/art.mjs`). The TikTok glyph in the "Follow us." links is the [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/?q=tiktok) TikTok path (CC0); it links only to MEON's own TikTok.

## Drink photos from free libraries

The home gallery's Drinks tab (Preston, 6 Nov 2026: "give drinks free images, and like Aiko matcha too"; ledger 571, 7 Nov 2026: free photos of Coke and the other drinks MEON sells, which replaces the 4 Nov "no other companies' packaging" order **for drinks only**). Each tile has one photo, a caption line under it, the group name, and MEON's own drinks from `data/catalogue.json` as text ("At MEON: Aiko Matcha (can)"). A photo is one of two kinds, and its caption line says which:

- **Exact product** ("Photo shows this drink"; the size stays in the "At MEON" line, since it isn't legible in the photo): the catalogue product itself, the same variant and pack. Taken only when the label is PD-textlogo (no illustrated packaging art), with one product as the subject and no people (Security's pre-rules, `boss-handoff/requests/security/20261107-0038-meon-571-drink-photo-prerules.md`).
- **Drink type** ("Photo shows the drink type"): a plain glass, cup or bowl of that kind of drink, no can, bottle, logo or brand in frame. A line under the grid says these tiles show the kind of drink, not the exact product.

CC0 and public-domain photos come first. CC BY / BY-SA photos are allowed for the drink tiles only (Security, 7 Nov 2026, overriding the 30 Oct no-credit rule for these files): each gets a credit line on its tile after the "At MEON" text, "Photo: <author>, <licence>, cropped", with the author linked to the Commons file page and the licence to its deed; the crop is released under the same licence.

Files are in `src/assets/img/drinks/`, cropped square by `scripts/make_drink_photos.py` (640 × 640 plus a 320 × 320 `-t320` copy, no EXIF or GPS) from each file's 1920 px Commons rendering (`water` from its 1573 px original; `coke-zero-sugar` and `chrysanthemum-tea` from their full originals, 4080 × 3060 and 1944 × 2592). Licences were read on each file's own Commons page: 6 Nov 2026 (19:41 PST; `iced-coffee` at 20:16 PST), and 7 Nov 2026 for `coke-zero-sugar` (00:52 PST) and `chrysanthemum-tea` (01:05 PST). Copies of the pages as checked are kept in MEON's private records (`boss-handoff/agents/meon-2/gallery/` and `agents/meon-2/drinks-571/`). `data/drink-photos.json` holds the same rows with the packaging reason and the SHA-256 of every shipped file.
