## Noodle and side photos from free libraries

Ledger 582 (Preston, 7 Nov 2026: every menu item without its own photo gets a free-licensed photo with a credit, or a written reason it has none). MEON's own photos always win: a noodle or side with a shoot or owner photo never shows a library photo, and the build stops if one is given both. The rules are Security's 582 pre-rules (`boss-handoff/requests/security/20261107-1811-meon-582-all-items-photo-prerules.md`) and its rulings of 7 Nov 2026; they allow CC0, public domain, the Pexels licence, and CC BY / BY-SA with a credit.

- **Two kinds, each with its caption under the photo.** "Photo shows this product, cooked plain": the same brand, product and flavour, photographed cooked with only its own sachets, from a series whose raw shot (linked below) shows the opened packet's contents, so the bowl can be checked against it (Security's six conditions). Only the cooked shot is used. "Photo shows the dish type": a plain photo of the same kind of dish (clear soup, red soup, curry, dry soy, dry chilli, cream, tomato, udon, milky broth), never passed off as the product or as a MEON bowl.
- **What a photo may show.** Nothing the dish lacks: no meat, seafood or egg unless the menu lists it, no peanuts, sesame or shellfish. A type photo with small toppings goes only to a noodle whose diet is meat (or seafood, for a seafood topping). No faces, other restaurants, watermarks or AI images, and no packet or pack art in frame.
- **Where they show.** Only where the caption and credit sit with them: the noodle cards (caption and a text credit), the noodle page (caption and a credit linking the source page and the licence) and the Pairings and sides page. The home page, the hero strip, search results, share images, structured data and `noodles.json` use MEON's own photos only.
- **Files.** `src/assets/img/noodles/free/<id>.webp` (900 × 900) with `sm/<id>.webp` (400 × 400), and `src/assets/img/toppings/free/<id>.webp` (480 × 480, or the original's size when it's smaller; never upscaled) with `-t384`, cut square by `scripts/make_free_photos.py`, which first checks each original's sha256. No EXIF, XMP or GPS. Several noodles share one type photo.
- **Records.** `data/free-photos.json` holds each photo's source page, licence, licence URL, author, the sha256 of the original as downloaded, the crop, the date checked, and which noodles and sides show it. `boss-handoff/agents/meon/free-images/SOURCES.md` adds the sha256 of every shipped file and what each photo shows; `INVENTORY.md` there lists every item and why the 17 noodles without a photo keep their packet art.

Licences were read on 7 Nov 2026 on each file's own Commons page or through the Commons API, and for Pexels on the photo's page ("Free download", linked to the Pexels License). Every photo is credited, including the CC0, public-domain and Pexels ones. Commons and Pexels pages can change later, so the date each was checked is in `data/free-photos.json`.

## Drink photos from free libraries

The home gallery's Drinks tab (Preston, 6 Nov 2026: "give drinks free images, and like Aiko matcha too"; ledger 571, 7 Nov 2026: free photos of Coke and the other drinks MEON sells, which replaces the 4 Nov "no other companies' packaging" order **for drinks only**). Each tile has one photo, a caption line under it, the group name, and MEON's own drinks from `data/catalogue.json` as text ("At MEON: Aiko Matcha (can)"). A photo is one of two kinds, and its caption line says which:

- **Exact product** ("Photo shows this drink"; the size stays in the "At MEON" line, since it isn't legible in the photo): the catalogue product itself, the same variant and pack. Taken only when the label is PD-textlogo (no illustrated packaging art), with one product as the subject and no people (Security's pre-rules, `boss-handoff/requests/security/20261107-0038-meon-571-drink-photo-prerules.md`).
- **Drink type** ("Photo shows the drink type"): a plain glass, cup or bowl of that kind of drink, no can, bottle, logo or brand in frame. A line under the grid says these tiles show the kind of drink, not the exact product.

CC0 and public-domain photos come first. CC BY / BY-SA photos are allowed for the drink tiles only (Security, 7 Nov 2026, overriding the 30 Oct no-credit rule for these files): each gets a credit line on its tile after the "At MEON" text, "Photo: <author>, <licence>, cropped", with the author linked to the Commons file page and the licence to its deed; the crop is released under the same licence.

Files are in `src/assets/img/drinks/`, cropped square by `scripts/make_drink_photos.py` (640 × 640 plus a 320 × 320 `-t320` copy, no EXIF or GPS) from each file's 1920 px Commons rendering (`water` from its 1573 px original; `coke-zero-sugar` and `chrysanthemum-tea` from their full originals, 4080 × 3060 and 1944 × 2592). Licences were read on each file's own Commons page: 6 Nov 2026 (19:41 PST; `iced-coffee` at 20:16 PST), and 7 Nov 2026 for `coke-zero-sugar` (00:52 PST) and `chrysanthemum-tea` (01:05 PST). Copies of the pages as checked are kept in MEON's private records (`boss-handoff/agents/meon-2/gallery/` and `agents/meon-2/drinks-571/`). `data/drink-photos.json` holds the same rows with the packaging reason and the SHA-256 of every shipped file.
